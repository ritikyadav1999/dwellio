# Dwellio UX Principles

## Core Philosophy

User time and energy should never be wasted.

The product should reduce:
- stress
- friction
- confusion
- operational fatigue

The experience should feel:
- effortless
- guided
- calm
- trustworthy

---

# UX Principles

## Hospitality Over Marketplace

Dwellio should feel like:
- a concierge experience
- a premium living service

NOT:
- a classified listings platform

---

## Emotional Calmness

Interfaces should reduce cognitive load.

Users should feel:
- emotionally safe
- organized
- reassured

Avoid:
- overwhelming choices
- dense interfaces
- aggressive CTAs

---

## Trust Before Utility

Trust should be communicated before operational complexity.

Examples:
- verified homes
- advisor support
- transparent pricing
- curated discovery

---

## Spacious Interfaces

Layouts should breathe.

Use:
- whitespace
- restrained hierarchy
- soft visual rhythm

Avoid:
- clutter
- crowded cards
- dashboard density

---

## Guided Experiences

Users should feel guided throughout:
- discovery
- scheduling
- onboarding
- relocation

Human support should always feel available.

---

## Premium Simplicity

Luxury means:
- fewer decisions
- less friction
- seamless movement
- emotional reassurance
