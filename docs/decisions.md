# Dwellio Product Decisions

## Landing Page

Decision:
No utility-heavy search bar.

Reason:
Landing page should communicate aspiration, trust, and premium positioning before operational discovery.

---

## Authentication

Decision:
OTP-first authentication.

Reason:
Lower friction, mobile-first onboarding, and better alignment with trust-based rental interactions.

---

## Dashboard

Decision:
Personal workspace instead of SaaS dashboard.

Reason:
Users should feel emotionally calm and guided rather than operationally overwhelmed.

---

## Concierge Positioning

Decision:
Strong advisor and concierge presence.

Reason:
Dwellio is a guided living experience, not only a property marketplace.

---

## UI Direction

Decision:
Calm luxury minimalism.

Reason:
Relocation is stressful. The product should reduce emotional fatigue and cognitive overload.

---

## Property Discovery

Decision:
Curated experiences over dense listing marketplaces.

Reason:
Quality and trust are prioritized over volume.
